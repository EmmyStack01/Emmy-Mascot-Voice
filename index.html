      Training script

      Lines to record if you train an AI voice on your own voice. Read each one in the mood shown.
      • Quiet room, mic about a hand-width from your mouth, no noise reduction or effects.
      • Bright, smiling and medium-quick. Keep the same distance and energy all the way through.
      • Leave a second of silence between lines. Record one long file, or one file per line named by its number.
      • Record it in one sitting if you can, so the voice stays consistent.

      Export

      Take the final audio from a voice actor or a speech service. These files carry Emmy's settings with them.

      Azure Speech lists Nigerian English voices under en-NG. Check the name against the voice list in your own account.

      Preview voices come from your browser and device, so they sound different on each one. Use them to check wording, pace and pronunciation. The finished Emmy audio comes from a voice actor, or a speech service reading the exports above.
